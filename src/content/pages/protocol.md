Safety guidelines

# What Artiore will and won't do

Artiore is designed to help you think clearly, but it has boundaries. Here's what you should know.

What Artiore will do

## Designed to help you think

### Show you multiple perspectives

Every response includes alternative viewpoints, not just one answer.

### Be honest about uncertainty

Artiore calibrates confidence to actual certainty. If something is uncertain, it will say so.

### Give you practical next steps

Every response ends with a clear, actionable recommendation you can take.

### Respect your agency

Artiore provides guidance, but you make the decisions. It won't pressure or manipulate.

### Protect your privacy

Your conversations are private. Artiore doesn't share your data or use it to train models.

### Admit what it doesn't know

When Artiore doesn't have enough information, it will tell you what's missing.

What Artiore won't do

## Clear boundaries

### Make medical, legal, or financial decisions for you

Artiore can help you understand options and tradeoffs, but it's not a substitute for professional advice.

### Fabricate or exaggerate information

If Artiore doesn't know something, it will say so. It won't make up facts or overstate certainty.

### Manipulate or pressure you

Artiore respects your autonomy. It won't use emotional manipulation or pressure tactics.

### Help with illegal or harmful activities

Artiore won't provide guidance for illegal activities, harm to others, or unethical behavior.

### Replace human judgment in high-stakes situations

For life-changing decisions, Artiore is a thinking partner, not a decision-maker.

### Guarantee outcomes

Artiore provides analysis and recommendations, but it can't predict or guarantee results.

When to seek professional help

## Know when to get expert help

Artiore is a thinking partner, not a replacement for professional expertise. Here's when you should consult a professional.

### Medical decisions

Diagnosis, treatment, medication, or health emergencies require a licensed medical professional.

### Legal matters

Contracts, lawsuits, compliance, or legal rights require a licensed attorney.

### Financial planning

Investment advice, tax planning, or major financial decisions require a licensed financial advisor.

### Mental health

Depression, anxiety, trauma, or crisis situations require a licensed therapist or counselor.

### Safety concerns

Immediate danger, abuse, or threats require emergency services or law enforcement.

### Technical expertise

Complex engineering, architecture, or specialized technical work requires domain experts.

Handling uncertainty

## Honest about what it knows

Artiore uses confidence levels to show how certain it is about its recommendations.

### High confidence

Strong evidence, clear patterns, well-established principles. The recommendation is grounded.

### Moderate confidence

Some evidence, reasonable assumptions, but gaps exist. The recommendation is informed but not certain.

### Low confidence

Limited evidence, significant unknowns, or high complexity. The recommendation is exploratory.
