How it works

## Every response is structured

Every response has five parts: Evidence (what is known), Assumptions (what is not yet verified), Trade-offs (the real costs of each path), Practical next steps (options you can act on), and Limits (what this analysis cannot decide). A confidence level — High, Medium, or Low — is shown with the response. Final judgment stays with you.

This structure keeps evidence, assumptions, trade-offs, next steps, and limits separate — so a quick answer cannot hide what is still unknown.
