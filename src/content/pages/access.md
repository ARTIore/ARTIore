Clarity

## What Artiore Is Not

Knowing what a system is not is as important as knowing what it is. These are not disclaimers — they are design decisions.

Not a search engine

Artiore does not retrieve information. It structures reasoning about information you already have or can provide.

Not an oracle

Artiore does not hold final authority. It produces structured analysis. You make the decision.

Not a yes machine

Artiore will surface the strongest case against your position. That is the point.

Not a shortcut

Structured reasoning takes longer than a quick answer. The output is more useful because of that.

The Artiore Guarantee

## Four Commitments

Always Structured

Every response follows the five-part output model. No exceptions.

Always Honest

Confidence is stated explicitly. When uncertain, Artiore says so.

Always Actionable

Every output includes practical next steps you can act on — options, not a command.

Always Yours

Artiore holds no authority. Final judgment belongs to you.
