# Sovereign Engine

Four-layer behavioral instrument.

Not a mystical relic. Not a magic 8-ball.

Abstract concepts — focus, intent, discipline — forced into the physical world through mechanics, accountability, and compounding metrics.

## Mechanized intent

Spirituality is discipline.

When you offer a kind word in Aurora, you are not thinking a nice thought — you are logging an action, gaining XP, and physically altering the acoustic environment. Your internal state has external consequences. The Engine makes them visible.

## Calibrate the decider

It does not decide. It audits.

Human decisions are ruined by emotional leakage, ego, and fatigue. The Witness layer measures what you have done — not how you feel.

- **STABLE** — Cleared to execute
- **HOLD** — Rebuild equity first

## Numeric anchor

Lock your target.

By inputting your specific digits — 2626, 1313 — you give your subconscious a numeric anchor. It filters the noise of the Ashen Plaza: the chaotic outside world that wants your attention scattered.

## The four layers

### Starwave — entrains your brain

Low-frequency oscillation forces your nervous system into calm focus.

Layer 01. Phases: Bow Inward, Inhale, Pulse, Exhale, Glow.

### Aurora — builds your equity

Requires active positive inputs to maintain its glow. Prevents cynicism.

Layer 02. Phases: Observe, Whisper, Reflect, Project.

Offer a word. It raises the kindness field and brightens the Aurora layer.

### Witness — audits your truth

Measures only what you have done. Level and streak. Keeps you honest.

Layer 03. Phases: Notice, Separate, Measure, Permit. Signal: STABLE or HOLD.

### Cipher — locks your target

Your numeric anchor. Filters the noise. Gives the subconscious direction.

Layer 04. Enter 4 digits (0–9). Each digit maps to a pulse intensity.
